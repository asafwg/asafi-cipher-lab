CRYPTOGRAPHY
SPECIFICATION
The presentation is Eurovision. The cryptography is deliberately conventional: AES-256-GCM with a key derived from your passphrase using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA-256.
ENCRYPTION
AES-256-GCM · 12-byte nonce · 128-bit authentication tag.
KEY DERIVATION
PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA-256 · 310,000 iterations · 16-byte random salt.
