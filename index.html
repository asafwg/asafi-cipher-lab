HOW IT WORKS

Your browser does the cryptography.

When you encrypt, the app creates a fresh random salt and AES-GCM nonce. Your passphrase is turned into a 256-bit encryption key with PBKDF2. The salt, nonce and authenticated ciphertext are packaged together so the same passphrase can decrypt it later.

1 — Derive

PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA-256 derives an AES-256 key from your passphrase and a random 16-byte salt.

2 — Encrypt

AES-256-GCM encrypts the text and authenticates it, detecting changes to the ciphertext.

3 — Package

The salt, nonce and authenticated ciphertext are encoded as Base64 or Hex for easy copying.

SPECIFICATION
